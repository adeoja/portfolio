SimAcademy

Designing the interface for a clinical simulation platform, from the live simulation screen to the learner dashboard, as the sole designer.

Project overview.

Role

UI/UX Design Intern and sole designer

Timeline

May to August 2026

Focus

Interface design and prototyping in Figma, for desktop and mobile

The challenge.

SimAcademy is a learning simulation platform made for medical programs, which give critical care simulations to their students to help them master high-stakes clinical decision making. During a simulation, a student has to read a changing patient state, keep track of what has already been done, and order the next action, all against a running clock. The interface needed to keep vitals, ongoing actions, assessments, and ordering in view together, and it needed to work on both desktop and phone.

What I designed.

  • A three-column simulation layout: patient monitor on the left, event log and actions hotbar in the center, and assessment, access and airway, handoff, and team assignment on the right.
  • The patient monitor, with a ventilator view.
  • The event log, with ongoing actions, a medication tracker, and lab results.
  • Action ordering: a search-first action bar, an action library, and a quick-access hotbar for prepped actions.
  • A medication prep queue that lets students prepare before the patient arrives.
  • A mobile layout built around four tabs: Monitor, Events, Assess, and Equip.
  • The learner dashboard and lesson attempts views.

Changes from design review.

Review feedback directly changed the desktop layout. The action search bar got a blue border so it reads as the primary input. The event log moved to the center, with a medication tracker tab so students can see what was given and when. The clock now shows clinical time only, placed near the action bar, and the status, team, and handoff panels moved to the right.
