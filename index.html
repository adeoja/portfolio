About me.

Hi, I'm Aarohan Deoja, a recent graduate of the University of Texas at Austin with a Bachelor of Science in Arts and Entertainment Technologies. Most of my coursework centered on game development, where I worked across programming, 3D modeling, and UI/UX design.

That UI/UX background led to a Summer 2026 internship at SimAcademy, an Austin-based medical tech startup. As the sole designer, I designed the interface for their simulation platform, which medical programs use to give their students practice with decision making in critical care situations. I also began designing an authoring tool that lets those programs build their own scenarios. Since completing the internship, I've been learning full-stack web development so I can contribute to front end development and more.
